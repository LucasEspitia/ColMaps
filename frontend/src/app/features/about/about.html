About the project

Exploring Colombia, through technology.

ColMaps is an academic web-based Geographic Information System focused on geographic exploration, performance, accessibility and reproducible software engineering.

About ColMaps

ColMaps combines open geographic data with modern web technologies to support the discovery of destinations across Colombia.

The project emphasizes modular architecture, efficient geographic visualization, accessibility and reproducibility.

Academic context

ColMaps is developed as a Bachelor's thesis at the Faculty of Informatics and Information Technologies (FIIT), Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava (STU).
